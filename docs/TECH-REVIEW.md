## /faults/ac-not-cooling: AC runs but does not cool
- Likely part on the diagram: The filter or the gas
- Answer: Often a dirty filter or low gas. You can clean the filter yourself once the AC is off. The gas and anything inside the unit is for a technician.
- Self-checks: Check the remote is on cool and the set temperature is below the room temperature. / Switch the AC off, take out the filter, clean it and let it dry before putting it back. / Close the doors and windows in the room. / Film a short video of the indoor and outdoor units running.
- Needs a technician: Low gas or a gas leak. / The compressor or the outdoor fan. / Anything that means opening the unit or touching wires.

## /faults/ac-leaking-water: AC leaking water
- Likely part on the diagram: The drain
- Answer: An AC drains water through a pipe. If it drips inside the room, the drain is usually blocked or the filter is dirty. Switch it off and send us a photo.
- Self-checks: Switch the AC off so the water stops. / Look at the drain hose outside the wall: is it kinked or blocked? / Clean the filter and let it dry. / Photograph where the water drips from the indoor unit.
- Needs a technician: A blockage inside the drain that you cannot reach. / The slope or fitting of the indoor unit. / Pipes icing over because of the gas.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/ac-freezing-up: AC freezing up
- Likely part on the diagram: The filter or the gas
- Answer: Ice on the unit is often a dirty filter blocking the air, or low gas. Switch the AC off and let it thaw, then clean the filter. If the ice comes back, call a technician.
- Self-checks: Switch the AC off and let the ice melt. / Clean the filter and let it dry. / Run it on fan only until it is dry. / If the ice returns, photograph it and send it to us.
- Needs a technician: Low gas or a gas leak. / The indoor fan not turning. / Ice that returns after the filter is clean.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/ac-loud: AC making noise
- Likely part on the diagram: The fan
- Answer: A loud AC can mean a dirty filter, something loose, or the fan. If you hear grinding or clicking, switch it off and send us a video with sound.
- Self-checks: Work out whether the noise comes from the indoor or the outdoor unit. / Clean the filter. / Make sure nothing is touching the outdoor unit. / Film a short video with sound.
- Needs a technician: Grinding or clicking from inside. / The fan or the compressor. / The mounting of the outdoor unit.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/ac-not-turning-on: AC will not turn on
- Likely part on the diagram: The control board
- Answer: Check the remote batteries and that the room has power. If the unit shows no light at all, the fault is usually the control board and needs a technician. Do not open the unit.
- Self-checks: Change the remote batteries. / Check the room has power and another device works. / See whether a light or a code shows on the indoor unit. / Photograph the light or code if there is one.
- Needs a technician: No light and no response from the unit. / Any burning smell or popping sound: switch it off at once. / Opening the board or checking the wiring.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/fridge-not-cooling: Fridge not cooling
- Likely part on the diagram: The compressor or the gas
- Answer: Keep the door shut so the cold stays in. The cause can be blocked airflow, low gas or the compressor. Gas and the compressor need a technician.
- Self-checks: Check the fridge is plugged in and the room has power. / Check the door closes fully and nothing is stuck in the seal. / Leave space behind and beside the fridge for air. / Move delicate food somewhere cold for now.
- Needs a technician: The compressor not running, or starting and cutting out. / Low gas or a gas leak. / The control board.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/fridge-leaking-water: Fridge leaking water
- Likely part on the diagram: The drain
- Answer: Water under a fridge is often a blocked defrost drain or a worn door seal. Unplug the fridge before touching the water around it and send us a photo.
- Self-checks: Unplug the fridge before wiping up water around it. / Check the door closes fully. / If there is a small drain hole inside at the back, clean it. / Photograph where the leak is.
- Needs a technician: A drain you cannot reach. / Frost that keeps building at the back. / Water near wires or the board.

## /faults/fridge-loud: Fridge making noise
- Likely part on the diagram: The fan or the compressor
- Answer: A soft hum is normal. A loud, grinding or constantly clicking noise can come from the fan or the compressor. Film a video with sound and send it to us.
- Self-checks: Check the fridge stands level and does not touch the wall. / Check nothing loose sits on top or behind. / Work out where the noise is: inside or at the back. / Film a short video with sound.
- Needs a technician: Constant grinding or clicking. / A noise that grows over time. / The fan or the compressor.

## /faults/washer-not-spinning: Washing machine not spinning
- Likely part on the diagram: The belt or the motor
- Answer: Make sure the clothes are spread out in the drum and no water is stuck inside. If it still will not spin, the fault is usually the belt, the motor or the board.
- Self-checks: Spread the clothes out in the drum and do not overload it. / Run the drain or spin cycle on its own. / Check the door is locked. / Photograph the screen if a code shows.
- Needs a technician: The drum will not turn at all. / Grinding noise or a burning smell. / The belt, the motor or the board.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/washer-not-draining: Washing machine not draining
- Likely part on the diagram: The drain pump
- Answer: Check the drain hose is not kinked or blocked and the wall drain is clear. Switch the machine off before checking anything. If water stays, the pump needs a technician.
- Self-checks: Switch the washer off and unplug it. / Check the drain hose is not kinked. / Check the wall drain outlet is not blocked. / Photograph the machine and the water inside.
- Needs a technician: Water stays after the hose is checked. / The pump hums but nothing drains. / Opening the machine or emptying the water from it.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/washer-error-code: Washing machine error code
- Likely part on the diagram: The control board
- Answer: Codes differ between brands and we do not guess them. Switch the washer off and on once. If the code returns, photograph it with the label and send it to us.
- Self-checks: Switch the washer off at the plug for a minute, then back on. / Photograph the screen and the code. / Photograph the label with the brand and model. / Send both photos to us on WhatsApp.
- Needs a technician: The code comes back. / A code together with a burning smell or a leak. / Any repair inside the machine.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/washer-shaking: Washing machine shaking
- Likely part on the diagram: The shock absorbers
- Answer: Usually the load is unbalanced or the machine is not level. Spread out the clothes and check the feet. If it keeps shaking or you hear knocking, the shock absorbers may need a technician.
- Self-checks: Stop the machine and spread the load evenly. / Check the machine stands level on the floor. / Do not wash a single heavy item on its own. / If the shaking continues, film it and send it to us.
- Needs a technician: Shaking that continues with a balanced load. / Heavy knocking while spinning. / The shock absorbers or inner parts.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/dryer-not-heating: Dryer not heating
- Likely part on the diagram: The heater
- Answer: Clean the lint filter and make sure the air vent is not blocked. If the dryer turns but gives no heat, the heater is usually at fault and needs a technician.
- Self-checks: Unplug the dryer and clean the lint filter. / Check the vent hose is not kinked or blocked. / Do not overload it. / Try a higher heat setting.
- Needs a technician: It turns but has no heat after the filter is clean. / The heater. / A burning smell: switch it off at once.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/dryer-not-drying-well: Dryer not drying well
- Likely part on the diagram: The lint filter
- Answer: Lint in the filter or the vent makes drying slower. Clean the filter and check the hose. If clothes are still damp, send us a video.
- Self-checks: Unplug the dryer and clean the lint filter. / Check the vent hose is not blocked. / Dry a smaller load. / Make sure the washer spins well before drying.
- Needs a technician: Clothes stay damp after the filter and vent are clear. / A vent blocked inside the wall. / Not enough heat.
- [ ] Reviewed by technician

## /faults/dryer-not-tumbling: Dryer not turning
- Likely part on the diagram: The belt
- Answer: Check the door is shut and the load is not too heavy. If you can hear the motor but the drum is still, the belt has probably snapped and needs a technician.
- Self-checks: Check the door is fully shut. / Reduce the load. / Unplug the dryer and start it again. / With it unplugged, turn the drum by hand; if it spins with no resistance, tell us.
- Needs a technician: The motor runs but the drum is still. / Grinding noise. / Opening the machine.
