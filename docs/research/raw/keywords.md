### 1. Gulf Arabic Search Dialect & Local Phrasing Analysis

In the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia (Dammam & Al-Khobar), user queries reflect specific linguistic, cultural, and commercial search habits:

* **Service intent terminology:** 
  * `صيانة` (Maintenance/Servicing): Used heavily for planned actions, cleaning, preventive check-ups, and corporate/formal requests.
  * `تصليح` (Fixing/Repair): Used when an appliance has broken down or failed.
  * `فني` (Technician) / `معلم` (Technician/Tradesman): Highly commercial "call now" search intent. Users search directly for `فني مكيفات` or `فني غسالات` to get an immediate phone/WhatsApp contact.
  * `شركة` (Company/Service Provider): Common for central AC projects, AMC contracts, or full-apartment AC duct cleaning.
* **AC terms:** 
  * `مكيف` (Singular unit) is overwhelmingly dominant for residential repair queries; `تكييف` is common for HVAC systems, central AC (`تكييف مركزي`), and commercial contracts.
  * `سبليت` (Split AC) vs `شباك` (Window AC): Both are ubiquitous in Dammam/Khobar apartments and older villas.
  * `تعبئة فريون` / `شحن فريون`: High-frequency search during early summer when ACs blow ambient air.
  * `تنظيف مكيفات` / `غسيل مكيفات`: Often paired with `في مكانه` (cleaning on-site without dismantling).
* **Major Appliances:**
  * `غسالة` vs `غسالات`: Users search `صيانة غسالات` (broad category) or `تصليح غسالة أوتوماتيك` / `فوق اتوماتيك` (front-load / top-load specific fault).
  * `نشافة` / `مجفف ملابس`: Distinct query line for standalone dryers.
  * `ثلاجة` vs `ثلاجات` & `فريزر`: High emergency intent because food spoilage is imminent in hot climates.

### 2. Seasonality & Demand Trends

* **Late Spring & Summer (April – September):** Extreme surge in AC emergency repairs, gas top-ups (`تعبئة فريون`), and capacitor/compressor failures due to high ambient temperatures and humidity in Dammam. Refrigerator faults (`الثلاجة لا تبرد`) also spike under extreme ambient load.
* **Pre-Summer (February – April):** Surge in preventive maintenance (`غسيل مكيفات`, `تنظيف دكت مكيفات`).
* **Autumn & Winter (October – January):** Demand pivots to washing machines, dryers (`نشافة`), water heaters/plumbing, and commercial kitchen refrigeration maintenance.
