<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro 7 static site, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, vanilla islands. Hosted on Hostinger with a small PHP file for leads (Ameer, 2 Nov 2026).

## Users

Households and small businesses in Dammam and nearby Eastern Province cities whose AC, fridge, freezer, washing machine or dryer has stopped working. They are on a phone, often in the heat, and they search in Arabic. They message on WhatsApp, often with a photo or video of the fault, or call when it is urgent. They worry about letting a stranger into the house and about being overcharged. English speakers are mostly expatriate residents.

## Product Purpose

Turn a person with a broken appliance into a WhatsApp chat, a call, a lead or a directions tap to the shop. Success is one of those four actions, counted in analytics.

## Positioning

A registered neighbourhood repair shop you can check, in a results page full of anonymous lead-generation sites. Named, located, one phone number, and it says only what is true.

## Capabilities and constraints

Three-step fault picker ending in a prefilled WhatsApp message. Call. Lead form to a Google Sheet with email. Directions. No online payment. No accounts. No AI chat.

## Brand commitments

Calm, careful, polite. A technician you would let into your home. Arabic is natural Saudi, spoken but respectful. English is a faithful second version, not a translation artefact.

## Evidence on hand

12 real job photos, 287 to 680 px wide, final. 8 Google reviews. Registration documents. Logo set. A real shop with an old sign showing a retired number, so the shopfront photo stays off the site until the new sign is up.

## Principles

1. If it cannot be checked, it is not on the page.
2. The phone number is never more than one thumb away.
3. Every page answers what a person typed, then gets out of the way.
